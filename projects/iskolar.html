01 /

Overview

A mobile UI concept for DOST Region VI’s iSKOLAR platform, designed to simplify access to scholarship services through a clean, intuitive, and mobile-first experience. The concept focuses on making scholarship information and services easier for students to access and navigate on mobile devices.

02 /

Problem

Scholarship information and services can be difficult to navigate when users have to go through different sources or interfaces to find what they need. Students need a more accessible way to discover scholarship opportunities, understand requirements, and access important scholarship-related services from one place.

03 /

Approach

I focused on translating the existing iSKOLAR platform into a mobile-first experience with a clear and straightforward interface. I organized scholarship information and key services around the needs of students, using simple navigation, clear content hierarchy, and accessible interactions to reduce unnecessary steps.

04 /

Solution

The resulting mobile UI concept presents iSKOLAR's scholarship services through a cleaner and more organized mobile interface. The design makes important information easier to discover while providing students with a more convenient way to navigate scholarship-related services from their mobile devices.
