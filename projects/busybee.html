Overview

BusyBee is a productivity application designed to help users organize their tasks and manage their daily activities in one place. The project focuses on providing a simple and structured interface for creating, organizing, and keeping track of tasks.

02 /

Problem

Managing multiple tasks can become difficult when information is scattered or when productivity tools feel too complicated. Users need a straightforward way to see what needs to be done, organize their priorities, and keep track of their progress.

03 /

Approach

I focused on creating a clean and intuitive interface that makes task management easy to understand and navigate. I designed the application's screens and user flows with clear visual hierarchy, organized task information, and accessible interactions in mind.

04 /

Solution

BusyBee provides a structured productivity experience where users can manage their tasks through a simple and visually organized interface. The final design prioritizes clarity, ease of navigation, and efficient task management while maintaining a friendly visual style.
