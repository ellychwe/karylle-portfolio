Overview

The Scholarship Information System was developed as a centralized platform for students and administrators to manage scholarship information, applications, and tracking.

The system was created to reduce the inconvenience of manually processing scholarship applications while providing students with a clearer way to monitor their application status.

02 /

Problem

Scholarship information and application processes can become difficult to manage when handled manually.

Students may have difficulty keeping track of available scholarships and application status, while administrators need an efficient way to manage scholarship records and applications.

03 /

Solution

I designed and developed a centralized scholarship platform where students can browse scholarships, submit applications, and track their application status.

Administrators can manage scholarship information, student applications, and scholarship records from one system.
